Content Studio

How to Use the App

A simple, step by step guide for reviewing and approving content

This app is your team's workspace for creating marketing content, reviewing it, and approving what goes out. It has four areas, shown as tabs down the left side: Brand Images, Content Engine, Approval Queue, and Admin. You do not need any technical knowledge to use it. The most important thing to know: nothing is ever posted to social media until a person approves it.

Please read first, for this test The social media connections (Instagram, Facebook, LinkedIn, YouTube) are not switched on yet, so they will be set up later. That means everything in the app works normally except the final "go live" step. When you click Approve, the app will try to publish and the item will show as failed in the queue. That is expected right now and is not a mistake. Once the social accounts are connected, Approve will post for real.

Signing in

Getting into the app
  1. Open the app link you were given in your web browser (Chrome or Edge work best).
  2. On the Content Studio sign in screen, type your email and password.
  3. Click Sign in. You will land on the Brand Images tab.

Top right of every screen you will see: a small Today $ amount (how much AI usage has cost today), a Generate button, and your initials. Click your initials any time to see your role or to Sign out.

1

Brand Images

Create a single branded graphic from a description

Use this when you want one image, for example an announcement graphic or a quote card.

  1. In the left column, click a Brand preset (this sets the style). If none exists, click + New preset and fill in a name and tone.
  2. Optional: under Avatar references pick a person to appear, and under Brand references pick a logo or product shot to guide the look.
  3. On the right, under Brand / page, choose which account this post is for.
  4. Under Platforms, click one or more (Instagram, Facebook, LinkedIn). One image and caption is made for each, sized correctly.
  5. In Describe the image, type what you want in plain words. You can also click + Attach reference to add a photo for this one image.
  6. Click Generate. Your image appears below under History and shows "Generating" for about 30 seconds, then updates by itself.
Refining an image

Each finished image has a row of buttons:

  • Edit: change only what you describe (for example "make the background navy") while keeping the rest the same.
  • Edit region: brush over the exact spot you want changed, then describe the change.
  • Regenerate: a fresh new take using a note you add. The layout may change.
  • Download: save the image to your computer.
  • Send to queue: when you are happy, this sends it to the Approval Queue for review.

Content Engine

Turn a recording into ready made posts, or generate a week at once
Option A: Generate a full week in one click
  1. At the top, find Generate this week's content and click Generate.
  2. Pick the Brand / page, then type a Theme for the week (for example "Spring product launch push").
  3. Click Confirm and generate week. It creates 10 image posts (Monday to Friday, Instagram and LinkedIn) and sends them to the Approval Queue.
Option B: Turn a webinar or video into clips and captions
  1. Under Ingest a video, drag a file onto the box or click to browse (video or audio, up to 2GB). Or paste a direct media link.
  2. Give it a Title, then click Ingest video.
  3. Watch the progress steps: Transcribe, then Analyze, then Rank. This can take a few minutes for a long video.
  4. When ready, the best moments appear as ranked segments with a score. Pick the Brand / page, click the segments you like, and choose the platforms.
  5. Optional: tick Create avatar hook (HeyGen) to also make a short talking video.
  6. Click Create content. The clips and captions land in the Approval Queue.

There is also a Competitors card where you can add competitors to keep an eye on. The app compiles a short weekly summary.

3

Approval Queue

The important one: review everything and decide what goes out

Everything created in the other tabs arrives here first. Nothing is published until you approve it.

  1. You will see a week board with cards grouped by day. Use the Platform and Status menus at the top to filter.
  2. Click any card to open a panel on the right with the full preview, the caption, and a history of who reviewed it.
  3. To send it out, click Approve. (During this test it will show as failed, as noted on the first page. That is expected until the social accounts are connected.)
  4. To send it back for changes, click Request edits, type what should change, and click Send for regeneration. The item is remade with your notes and returns here.
  5. If an item shows as failed, open it and click Retry to send it back for a fresh review.
Planning ahead: the content calendar
  1. Scroll to Content calendar and click + Plan a day.
  2. Choose a Date, the Brand / page, the Platforms, and type a Prompt for the day.
  3. Click Add to calendar. It will generate automatically each morning and appear here for approval.
  4. To try it right away, click Generate now next to any planned day.

Admin

Costs and system health, for administrators

This tab shows how much the AI tools have cost and whether everything is running smoothly.

  • Spend for today and the last 7 days, broken down by tool and task.
  • A list of recent runs with the cost of each.
  • System health, including anything that failed and needs a second look.

This tab is visible to administrators only.

Good to know

Quick answers
Can I accidentally post something?
No. Nothing goes public until someone approves it in the Approval Queue, and only admins and reviewers can approve.
Why does it say "failed" after I approve?
Because the social media accounts are not connected yet for this test. Everything else worked. Once they are connected, approving will publish for real.
Does creating images cost money?
Each generation uses a small amount of AI credit. You can see the running total at the top right (Today $) and the full breakdown on the Admin tab. There is a daily spending limit built in.
How long does an image take?
Usually around 30 seconds. The page updates on its own, so you can just wait and watch.
What are the two brands?
The accounts you have set up. You choose which one each post is for so the captions and styling match.
